# ARCADIA

A 3D Mars habitat management simulator for kids aged 7 to 12, made by Team **Tronador** for the [Build a Junior Astronaut Mission Trainer](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/2026/challenges/junior-astronaut-mission-trainer/) challenge of the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026.

The player builds a base out of **hemispherical** and **half-cylinder** domes joined together, and has to balance energy, oxygen, water, food, sanity and radiation protection. No decision is free: every machine uses energy, and there is never enough energy for everything.

The game is available in **Spanish, English and Italian** (Options › Game › Language).

## How to play

Open `index.html` in a browser (nothing to install: everything, including Explore mode, the images and the 3D models, is inside that single file). It also works from GitHub Pages. It needs an internet connection for the 3D engine and the NASA Mars Trek maps.

When it opens, the **TRONADOR** mission insignia shows for 3 seconds and then the menu appears, following the `Estructura de juego.drawio` diagram. The whole program uses Helvetica Bold and robotic, space-themed sounds generated by the browser.

- **Play**
  - **Survival:** in «Choose your mission» you pick **Cadet** or **Commander** (they change the starting resources, how often extreme weather happens and the penalties), then **Start** (with the dog's missions) or **Free mode** (no guided missions). After confirming, you type your name (with the keyboard or the controller's on-screen keyboard): the assistant dog calls you by your name. Then comes an orbital descent over Arcadia Planitia using real Mars Trek maps, inspired by the map loading in Star Wars Battlefront, and a 10-second timelapse in which the base is built.
  - **Explore:** a 3D globe of Mars with the real NASA Mars Trek mosaic spins to each of the 5 destinations (Olympus Mons, Korolev Crater, Jezero Crater, Mount Sharp and Valles Marineris). Pressing **Start mission** plays the same orbital descent followed by the rover landing. Every place has a **half-buried football** to find.
- **Load game:** 3 save slots stored in the browser.
- **Options:** Game (language and controls guide at start), Video (quality and camera sensitivity), Audio (music and effects) and Controls (remap keys and buttons). It is the same screen from the menu and from the pause menu; go back with `Esc` or `B`.
- **Credits** (team members and the use of AI tools) and **Exit game**.

You can play with keyboard and mouse or with a **USB Xbox 360 controller**: the game detects it as soon as you press any button. With the controller, a crosshair appears in the middle of the screen for aiming. Default keys and buttons:

| Action | Keyboard and mouse | Xbox 360 controller |
|---|---|---|
| Move around the map | `W` `A` `S` `D` or arrows, or drag | Left stick |
| Turn / tilt the camera | `Q` `R`, or right click + drag | Right stick |
| Zoom | Mouse wheel or `Z` `X` | `RT` zoom in · `LT` zoom out |
| Confirm (Enter) | `Enter` or left click | `A` |
| Cancel (Esc) | `Esc` or right click | `B` |
| Build mode (helmet) | `B` | `Y` |
| Heart mode (wellbeing) | `H` | `X` |
| Rotate the object | `E` | `LB` / `RB` |
| Short details of the selected object | `Y` | `Y` (in build mode, with an object selected) |
| Move / rotate a building | `G` or the «Move» button | Hold `A` on it |
| Dismantle | `0` or `Del` | Hold `Y` on it |
| Choose HUD buttons (statistics, pause, ×1 ×3 ×10, Objectives, panels) | Click | D-pad ↑ ↓ ← → (only moves the focus) + `A` |
| Ice radar · Show the mission · Map | `9` · `O` · `M` | `R3` · `Back` · `L3` |
| Pause menu | `P` or `Esc` | `Start` |

One sol (Martian day) lasts 10 minutes of play at ×1 speed. When each mission finishes loading, a guide with the controls appears (keyboard or controller). While playing with a controller, the HUD shows the Xbox button for each part; with keyboard and mouse those hints are hidden (the full list is in Options › Controls).

**Building:** every structure can be up to 3 squares away from the base; domes that are lined up are joined automatically by long tunnels. Everything (including the starting base) can be moved, rotated or dismantled. Before building, «Are you sure?» appears. In Free mode building costs no budget. The statistics at the top are grouped into **HOUSING** (water, oxygen, food, energy, protection and budget) and the **player's name** (sanity, wellbeing and health). The build panel slides in from the left and the white **BUILD MODE** tab drops from the box at the top right. Every build order is carried out by three humanoid robots that assemble it in 5 seconds. Also included: a **Research habitat** (desks, ergonomic chairs, lights and computers), **huge methane tanks** (they replace batteries: spare energy is stored as methane) and **5 gas tanks** of 0.6 × 1.90 m (O₂, N₂, CH₄, CO₂ and CO). The Control Module has no beds (the base starts with a dormitory). Every module has a security camera outside and, at the same height inside, a screen that shows the outside in real time. The KRUSTY reactor, the ice drill (auger with tripod), the furniture and the cameras are the team's 3D models.

## Interface and mechanics

- **Top left:** 9 live circular indicators: Water, Oxygen, Food, Energy, Protection, Sanity, Health, Budget and **Astronaut wellbeing**.
- **Top right:** sol and time, temperature, weather, time speed and Objectives.
- **Bottom left:** the **heart** opens wellbeing and the **helmet** turns on **build mode** (category bar, orange sign and black grid). Each category opens a vertical panel on the right-hand side, in a single column, with Hemisphere 2×2 and Half-cylinder 3×2 as their own options.
- **Wellbeing:** 5 pillars (exercise with ARED, T2 and CEVIS; nutrition and hydration; medical and SANS monitoring; sleep and circadian rhythm; psychological support). Each one needs facilities and uses resources; if they are neglected, health and sanity drop.
- **Weather:** Mild, Blizzard (panels at 20 %, more heating, nobody goes out) and Tornado (it can damage outdoor structures if the anchors are not turned on; afterwards it leaves the panels clean).
- **Missions:** the astronaut dog appears with every mission in the format «(Category) Objective / Description / Reward».
- **Buildings:** when selected, an outline in the colour of their sector is drawn. When the camera zooms in (top view), domes turn see-through and show their interior; the hydroponic farm is an aquaponic system with a fish tank, fish, pumps and stepped growing trays.
- **Pause (`P`):** blurs the background; Resume, Save game (3 slots), Load game, Options, VR mode (coming soon), Weather (free mode only) and Exit with confirmation.

## Base location: Arcadia Planitia

The base is in the east-central part of **Arcadia Planitia**, at **40.0° N · 190.0° E** (170.0° W): a low plain in the northern hemisphere with water ice close to the surface. The nearest impact site with confirmed ice (Dundas et al., 2014) is about 54 km away, at 39.1° N · 190.2° E.

The **Arcadia map** (`M` key or `L3` on the controller) shows real maps from [NASA Mars Trek](https://trek.nasa.gov/mars/) without external libraries:

| Layer | Source | What it is for |
|---|---|---|
| Colour | Viking MDIM 2.1 (232 m/px) | Seeing the ground as from orbit |
| Terrain | MOLA + HRSC (200 m/px) | Understanding heights: Arcadia is low |
| Infrared | THEMIS day (100 m/px) | Telling rock from dust |
| Geology | USGS SIM 3292 | Terrain types and ages |
| Thermal inertia | MGS TES | Rock, packed soil or ice vs. loose dust |

On top are drawn the base, the **impact sites with exposed ice** and the **new craters** in the area (queried live from the Mars Trek ArcGIS service, with a saved backup list) and the Expedition mode destinations. It needs internet: the maps do not load inside a Claude artifact, but they do on GitHub Pages or when opening the file.

## Mechanics

- **Grid building:** every new dome has to be joined to another one. Outdoor structures need an airlock.
- **Energy:** solar panels (daytime only), the KRUSTY reactor and methane storage with the R-SOFC cell. If energy runs short, machines shut down by priority.
- **Oxygen:** MOXIE + electrolysis, which also makes methane.
- **Water:** ice under the surface (radar and rover), drills and the water plant.
- **Food:** hydroponic farm with tilapia and microalgae, kitchen and supplements that arrive from Earth.
- **Protection:** regolith shielding and a shelter during solar storms.
- **Communications:** a 4 to 24 minute delay with Earth. Without an antenna, no rockets or storm warnings arrive.
- **Sanity and health** of the crew.

## Explore mode (rover + drone)

It is inside `index.html`, on the same page (no frames or separate files; `expedicion.html` only redirects there). A Perseverance-style rover explores Olympus Mons, Korolev Crater, Jezero Crater, Mount Sharp and Valles Marineris. You play with the keyboard or the Xbox 360 controller (left stick or `RT`/`LT` to drive, `A` analyse, `X` camera, `Y` Ingenuity, `Start` pause). It carries the **Ingenuity** helicopter under its belly: you can deploy it to see each place from the air and take aerial photos of the targets.

| Action | Rover | Ingenuity |
|---|---|---|
| Move | `W` `S` forward / back · `A` `D` turn | `W` `S` forward / back · `A` `D` turn |
| Up / down | — | `R` or `Space` up · `F` or `Shift` down and land |
| Action | `E` analyse with an instrument | `E` aerial photo with the RTE camera (above 4 m) |
| Camera | `C` follow / Navcam | `C` follow / NAV (downward, B/W) / RTE (colour) |
| Ingenuity | `I` deploy · `V` switch to the helicopter | `V` back to the rover (only on the ground) |

**Real terrain:** each place's terrain comes from elevation models measured from orbit, not made-up formulas: Jezero with NASA Mars Trek's 20 m CTX model (centred on Perseverance's landing site), Mount Sharp with the 1 m HiRISE model of the edge of Gale's mound, Olympus Mons and Korolev Crater with Mars Global Surveyor's MOLA laser altimeter (463 m, the global model shown by Mars Trek), and Valles Marineris with the 24 m CTX model of Coprates Chasma, on the edge of the south wall (13.76° S · 66.54° W), with more than 3 km of real drop into the canyon. You can see up to 12 km around (180 km in Valles Marineris, Olympus Mons and Korolev). Only centimetre-scale roughness is added on top. The scripts that prepared the data are in `herramientas/terreno-real/`.

Limits taken from the real Ingenuity: maximum altitude of 24 m (its record), 10 m/s, about 170 s per flight, rotors at 2,537 rpm and a 1 km range from the rover. It needs flat ground to land. Solar charging is sped up. Ingenuity only flew in Jezero (72 flights, 2021–2024); in the other places the flight is a simulation. Sources: Wikipedia, articles *Ingenuity (helicopter)* and *Perseverance (rover)*.

## Data used

- **Project data (team PDF):** consumption and production of each module, heating and lighting formulas, stoichiometry of electrolysis and CO hydrogenation, diet and growing areas.
- **NASA DONKI:** real dates of solar energetic particle (SEP) events from 2024–2025, used in the solar storm warnings. They are archived data, not live.
- **Curiosity RAD:** surface dose of ~0.67 mSv per sol.
- Full sources inside the game, in the **Learn** section.

Values added for the game (not from the PDF): 0.84 kg of O₂ and 4 L of net water per astronaut per sol, building costs, drill production and the intensity of solar storms (exaggerated so their effect is noticeable).
